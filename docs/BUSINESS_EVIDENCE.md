3. Real product range, prices, stock, halal and allergen information.
4. Payment methods, delivery areas and fees, pickup process, returns policy.
5. Opening hours and outlet list.
6. Official website and social links before linking to them.
7. No third-party reviews or testimonials are reproduced; any future testimonials need consent.
